Our Story

How It All Began

Like all great modern romances, ours began on Hinge in 2022 — because apparently that's where destiny lives now. We went on a few dates, and while things were promising, Hemin hit a bit of a "what do I actually want?" phase.

Nikhyta, on the other hand, had no such confusion… and swiftly promoted him to the friend zone. Efficient. Decisive. Necessary.

"The right person at the wrong time is just the right person… who needed a second interview."

To his credit, Hemin took it like a champ. No drama, no disappearing act — just consistently being the genuinely lovely human he'd always been. So they stayed in touch here and there, proving that not all almost-romances end in chaos.

July 2023 — Round Two

Hemin spotted his opportunity when Nikhyta was back in London for a friend's wedding and asked her out to dinner. Round two: surprisingly good. Suspiciously good. But Nikhyta wasn't fully sold just yet.

Notting Hill Carnival — The Test

When Hemin suggested meeting again, Nikhyta made it clear — she was busy, booked, and thriving. So he did what any determined man would do: he joined her and her friends at Notting Hill Carnival. Bold move. Her friends wasted no time putting him in the hot seat. This time, Hemin came prepared. Clear intentions, no confusion, no friend zone relapse.

And somewhere in between…

Between the music, crowds, and chaos — between persistence, good timing, and passing the ultimate friend-interrogation test — things finally clicked. Now here we are.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything else you might be wondering. If your question is not here, reach out!

What is the dress code?+
Black tie preferred, formal attire minimum. We want everyone to feel glamorous and celebrated. Please avoid white, ivory, or cream out of respect for the bride. Ladies may wish to bring a light wrap for the evening.
Can I bring children?+
We adore your little ones. However, this will be an adult-only celebration. Exceptions are made for infants who are still breastfeeding — please contact us before submitting your RSVP so we can make the appropriate arrangements.
Are there any photography restrictions?+
We have hired a wonderful professional photographer to capture every magical moment. We kindly ask that guests refrain from taking photos during the ceremony so we can all be fully present. After the ceremony, please snap away and share with #HeminAndNikhyta2027!
Is there a gift list?+
Your presence truly is the greatest gift. However, if you would like to contribute, we have a small wish list and a honeymoon fund available. Details were included with your invitation. Cash gifts are also gratefully received.
Will there be vegetarian and vegan options?+
Absolutely. Our caterers have prepared a full menu with options for dietary requirements, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and halal. Please indicate your needs in your RSVP so we can ensure you are looked after.
How do I get in touch with a question not covered here?+
Please reach out to our wedding coordinator, Jayna, at wedding@heminandnikhyta.com — she will be happy to help with any further questions. We cannot wait to celebrate with you!
