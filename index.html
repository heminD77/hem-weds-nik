Our Story

How It All Began

Like all great modern romances, ours began on Hinge in 2022 — because apparently that's where destiny lives now. We went on a few dates, and while things were promising, Hemin hit a bit of a "what do I actually want?" phase.

Nikhyta, on the other hand, had no such confusion… and swiftly promoted him to the friend zone. Efficient. Decisive. Necessary.

"The right person at the wrong time is just the right person… who needed a second interview."

To his credit, Hemin took it like a champ. No drama, no disappearing act — just consistently being the genuinely lovely human he'd always been. So they stayed in touch here and there, proving that not all almost-romances end in chaos.

July 2023 — Round Two

Hemin spotted his opportunity when Nikhyta was back in London for a friend's wedding and asked her out to dinner. Round two: surprisingly good. Suspiciously good. But Nikhyta wasn't fully sold just yet.

Notting Hill Carnival — The Test

When Hemin suggested meeting again, Nikhyta made it clear — she was busy, booked, and thriving. So he did what any determined man would do: he joined her and her friends at Notting Hill Carnival. Bold move. Her friends wasted no time putting him in the hot seat. This time, Hemin came prepared. Clear intentions, no confusion, no friend zone relapse.

And somewhere in between…

Between the music, crowds, and chaos; between persistence, good timing, and successfully passing the ultimate friend-interrogation test, things finally clicked. What started as occasional messages became daily conversations. Dinner dates turned into weekends together. Soon, they were building a life full of adventures, laughter, long drives, family gatherings, and the kind of comfort that comes from knowing you've found your person. As it turned out, the second interview went exceptionally well.

The Proposal

Two years after giving love another chance, Hemin had one more plan up his sleeve. On a warm summer evening at Sandown Racecourse, surrounded by thousands of glowing candles and under a sky lit by a spectacular drone show, Nikhyta thought she was simply enjoying a magical night out. As the final display illuminated the sky and the last drones formed their closing masterpiece, Hemin got down on one knee. In a moment filled with happy tears, complete surprise, and probably at least one shocked expression, he asked the question that had been building ever since that second chance began. Thankfully for him, Nikhyta said YES! And just like that, the boy who once found himself in the friend zone became her fiancé.

Blessings & Celebrations

A few months later, surrounded by family, friends, love, and enough food to feed a small village, we celebrated our Hindu engagement ceremony. The day was filled with blessings, traditions, laughter, colourful outfits, countless photographs, and the joining together of two families. It was a beautiful reminder that while our story may have started with a dating app, it grew into something much greater, supported by the people who mean the most to us.

The Next Chapter

From Hinge to the friend zone. From the friend zone to Notting Hill Carnival. From second chances to a candlelit proposal beneath the stars. Our journey has never been quite traditional, but then again, neither are we.

And this is only the beginning… We can't wait to celebrate the next chapter of our story with all of you.

Our Ceremony

The Hindu Wedding Ceremony

A Hindu wedding is far more than a single moment — it is a rich tapestry of rituals, prayers, and blessings that have been carried through generations. Each element of our ceremony holds deep meaning, weaving together family, faith, and commitment into something truly sacred. We hope this guide helps you follow along and feel part of every beautiful moment.

The Rituals
Ganesh Puja

A prayer to Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles, carried out by the bride's parents seeking blessings for a smooth and happy wedding.

Baraat

The Baraat is the groom's wedding procession, where Hemin arrives surrounded by family, friends, music, dancing, and enough energy to convince an entire street to join in. Modesty is not part of the itinerary.

Ponkhana

A traditional welcome ritual where the bride's mother greets the groom and symbolically accepts him into the family.

Antarpat

A cloth is held between the bride and groom before they first see each other, symbolising the transition from two separate lives to one shared journey.

Bride's Entrance

Accompanied by her loved ones, the bride makes her entrance to meet the groom and begin the ceremony.

Jaimala (Varmala)

Once the Antarpat is lowered, the bride and groom exchange flower garlands as a symbol of mutual acceptance and respect.

Hasta Milap

The couple's hands are joined together, symbolising the union of their two lives and families.

Kanyadaan

The bride's parents formally entrust their daughter to her new partnership in marriage.

Vivah Havan

Sacred prayers are offered around a holy fire, which serves as a witness to the marriage.

Mangal Pheras

One of the most important parts of a Hindu wedding. The couple circles the sacred fire four times, making promises of love, support, and commitment with the fire acting as a divine witness to their marriage. Each round represents a different commitment that will guide their life together.

Saptapadi (Seven Steps)

The bride and groom take seven symbolic steps together, each representing a shared vow for married life — including friendship, trust, respect, family, happiness, and lifelong commitment.

Sindoor & Mangalsutra

The groom places sindoor (vermilion) and a sacred necklace on the bride as traditional symbols of their marriage.

Akhand Saubhagyavati

A traditional Hindu blessing wishing a married woman lifelong happiness, prosperity and the blessing of her husband's long life.

Kansar

The couple feed each other sweets, symbolising the sharing of life's joys and challenges together.

Ashirwad

Family and friends offer their blessings and best wishes for the couple's future.

Vidaai

An emotional farewell as the bride leaves with the groom to begin the next chapter of their lives as husband and wife.
